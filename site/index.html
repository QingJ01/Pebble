Privacy First

Your inbox is nobody else's business

Mail, search index and attachments stay on your device. No telemetry. Pebble only connects to your mail servers and the optional services you turn on.

Encrypted Credentials

Passwords and OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a per-device key.

100% Local Storage

SQLite database, search index, and attachments — all on your device.

Trackers Blocked

Remote images and tracking pixels stay blocked until you allow them, per message or per sender.

Open Source

AGPL-3.0 licensed. Inspect every line of code yourself.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Thoughtfully designed around how you actually work with email.

Unified Inbox

Every account in one list, each message marked with its account. Narrow it to one account in a click.

Your Providers

Sign in to Gmail and Outlook, or use IMAP and POP3. Server settings fill in for about 30 common providers.

Kanban Board

Turn mail into tasks across To do, Waiting and Done, with a note on each card.

Undo Send & Scheduling

Take a message back within seconds or schedule it for later. Queued mail survives restarts.

Folders & Drag-and-Drop

Create folders and move mail by dragging it onto the sidebar. Every move can be undone.

Fast Local Search

Full-text search on your device, filtered by sender, recipient, date, attachments or folder.

Rules & Snooze

Rules sort new mail as it arrives; snooze brings a message back when you need it.

Translation

A bilingual reading view with DeepL or an LLM of your choice.

Keyboard & Themes

Customizable shortcuts, light and dark themes, and dark mode for HTML mail.
